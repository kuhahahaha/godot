1. **P1 — A failing or crashing test can be hidden by `tee`** (`runner.yml` lines 55–59). This step has no explicit shell. GitHub's documented Linux default is `bash -e {0}`, not `bash -eo pipefail`; under that shell `false | tee /dev/null` continues with rc 0. Therefore a test executable failure or ASan termination may be replaced by `tee`'s successful status. A local fake child emitted a one-case, one-failure doctest summary and exited 1; the pipeline, actual candidate regex and whole step still exited 0. Set an explicit `shell: bash` or `set -o pipefail` before the pipelines, and test a failing child as well as a successful one.
