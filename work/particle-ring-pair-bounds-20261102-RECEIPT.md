# Particle ring radius pair bounds, 2026-11-02

The CPU 2D/3D emitters and `ParticleProcessMaterial` previously accepted an inner ring radius greater than the outer radius, or lowered the outer radius below the inner radius. Their ring emission formulas use the squared difference directly. The three public setter pairs now reject either invalid transition without publishing it. CPU/GPU conversion resets the destination inner radius to zero before applying a valid source pair, so lowering a previously larger destination pair remains possible.
