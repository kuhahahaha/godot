# Particle ring radius pair reconciliation, 2026-11-03

The earlier pair-rejection implementation in `c86e683be3` kept `inner <= outer` for direct setters, but prevented a valid saved `CPUParticles2D` ring with outer `0.5` and inner `0.4` from reloading. A new node starts at outer `1.0` and inner `0.8`; PackedScene applies the saved outer first, and the setter rejected `0.5` against the default inner `0.8`. The real `ring_bounds.gd` SceneTree control reproduced this with rc 1 and `2D ring scene roundtrip lost valid radii` before the repair.

The CPU 2D/3D and `ParticleProcessMaterial` setters now reconcile the paired radius atomically: increasing inner raises outer when needed; decreasing outer lowers inner when needed. The material setter also publishes the adjusted partner uniform to RenderingServer. Non-finite, negative and squared-overflow inputs remain rejected. The fixture checks both reconciliation directions across all three public objects, CPU↔GPU conversion in 2D/3D, and the saved low-radius 2D scene roundtrip.
