## Implementation and evidence

- `mesh_surface_set_material` refuses changing an Indexed144 external surface's retained material RID; the same RID is a no-op. Ordinary mesh material updates remain on their original path.
- `mesh_add_external_surface` requires the supplied RID to be owned by the actual MaterialStorage and to resolve to an initialized material. This is a point-in-time native check, not a material-contract lease or E12 qualification.
- Actual existing shader owners' material generations advance on shader source and default-texture changes. Every owner is checked for generation saturation before mutation, and all are advanced before the existing notification phase; newly allocated MaterialData receives its owner's generation. No renderer admission authority is generated.
