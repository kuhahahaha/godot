## Source findings

1. **Actual graph directions are preserved.** `after/.../rendering_device_immutable_hold.cpp:365` enumerates forward-map edges only when bidirectional, reverse-map edges in both modes, then shader embedded samplers and texture owner. Actual acquisition calls it with `false` at line 438 and actual staging calls it with `true` at line 1143. The old acquisition and staging loops have the same directions and ordering. Staging retains rollback on visitor failure; acquisition retains its prepublication error return. `_add_dependency(id, dependency)` places the dependent in `dependency_map[dependency]`, so the names are not being interpreted backwards.
2. **Embedded links remain directed.** Shader-to-sampler and texture-to-owner links are traversed from their owning node; the patch does not add a global reverse scan. This matches the actual staging graph, even in bidirectional mode. Calling that graph fully undirected would be inaccurate, but the implementation map explicitly makes this distinction.
3. **Virtual identity is typed and separate from RID identity.** The line 849 traversal validates actual/future key shape, deduplicates the complete key, and uses symbol ordinals without inventing owner RIDs. Future VAs expand only owned vertex buffers; future pipelines require exactly one owned shader. Actual supported owner existence is checked. Incoming virtual edges are followed only in bidirectional mode, including a future VA reached late through another VA's backing. Future factory identity/coalescing and exact ready-shader/descriptor proofs are caller obligations, not supplied by this helper.
4. **Bound and publication behavior are sound within this helper.** `VectorView::size()` is uint32; `Vector`/`CowData::Size` is int64. The uint64 comparison against the uint32 limit precedes checked `resize(index + 1)`, so growth cannot wrap at the permitted bound. Copying the current key avoids a reference invalidated by append. Failure leaves output empty; success moves the local result. These are node-count/allocation guarantees, not eligibility, reservation, execution-time, or aggregate pin guarantees.
5. **The actual stage predicate is preserved.** Line 955 extracts disposal owner/incarnation/driver/counter checks, the original qualified-use relationships, uniform-set callback ownership, and transfer-worker checks. It does not silently expand acquisition policy onto adoption-only neighbors. In particular, full immutable eligibility and original render-pipeline qualification policy remain where they were. Primitive metadata is committed only after success.
6. **Callback origins remain local to the original hold group.** Stage validates its up to two real holds, copies their material/generation records at lines 1043–1046, and supplies only that batch to the extracted predicate. The matching condition still requires actual set provenance plus `_native_hold_callback_is_owned`, including the actual material-owned set record. The predicate does not invoke the callback or acquire a new lock. There is no intervening callback/mutation in this staging scope that would make the origin copy stale relative to the old loop. A future orchestrator must preserve these per-group origins; a union of unrelated material origins would change authorization.
7. **Locking is unchanged for the production refactor.** Acquisition already owns the RD mutex before the shared enumerator. The stage/predicate methods retain their private `_locked` convention and introduce no Surface-version acquisition, factory, registry mutation, or new callback. The prospective helper also requires its caller to retain the RD graph lock and any original-owner lifetime protections; its suffix does not acquire or authenticate them.

## Explicit integration and evidence limits

These are remaining work, not defects newly introduced by this prerequisite patch:

- The typed closure has only its definition and focused test as callers. There is no authenticated preparation-scope orchestration invoking it before factories.
- There is no exact reservation-layout validator, original pin/row/request authentication, complete retained root builder, virtual factory identity coalescing, material raw-root limit proof, full acquisition eligibility pass, combined per-resource/device hold increment proof, or both-groups-plus-owned-pipeline admission in this patch.
- The extracted predicate widens `use_count + binding_used` for its own use scan. The surrounding old stage/adopt arithmetic still uses uint32 addition, and reservation traversal/capacity invariants remain outside this extraction. Therefore this slice cannot support a claim that request capacity/counter arithmetic has been generally repaired. The implementation map correctly records that gap.
- The test at `after/tests/servers/rendering/test_mesh_storage_external.cpp:270` independently exercises a useful actual/future VA bridge, exact count/refusal, reverse-only direction, and selected no-effect counters/maps. It does not execute actual hold acquisition or staging through their new common path, future pipelines, directed special links, qualified-use/callback authorization, reservation boundaries, or aggregate counter saturation. Runtime regression coverage of the changed production paths remains necessary before semantic acceptance; syntax alone cannot supply it.
- No real-backend or GPU claim follows from this review or the fake-driver test.

The implementation map accurately separates the delivered slice from the remaining gate. It is suitable to continue as a prerequisite with that boundary intact; it is not sufficient to mark prospective G rejection complete.
