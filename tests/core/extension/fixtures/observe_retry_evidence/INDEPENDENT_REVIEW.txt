Concurrency/custody reasoning

FIFO schedule uses the same mutex, sets QUEUED before publication, and restores PENDING on refusal; therefore a published turn cannot be misclassified as unpublished. The subsequent exact-serial consume revalidates state/identity. A PENDING state may become RETURNED between initial lookup and schedule, but original schedule does not republish non-PENDING; conservatively Busy then later consume is safe.
