The fixture disables ordinary rendering before queuing capture and readback but never requests subsequent draws. main/main.cpp around 5267 invokes RenderingServer::draw only when wants_present or RD::has_pending_resources_for_processing is true. The latter tests frames_pending_resources_for_processing, which is maintained for deferred resource frees; buffer_get_data_async merely queues a graph copy/readback request and does not set this counter. The async callback is delivered when the normal frame path revisits its submitted frame after fence completion. Therefore success depends on incidental startup pending frees providing enough frame advances. With no pending disposal work, the capture/readback can remain unsubmitted or unretired and the test times out despite correct rendering.
